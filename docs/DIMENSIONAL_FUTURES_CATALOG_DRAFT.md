## Creation gates

Before creating these products in Shopify:

1. Select suppliers and exact blank products.
2. Confirm variants, sizes, colors, production locations, and shipping regions.
3. Order physical samples.
4. Approve exact material, fit, texture, print, care, and durability statements.
5. Complete paid community review for the messages represented.
6. Calculate costs, returns allowance, contribution amount, and margin before setting prices.
7. Produce and approve original product artwork and accurate mockups.
